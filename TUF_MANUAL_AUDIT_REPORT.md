# TUF ACADEMY TRAINING MANUALS — COMPREHENSIVE FIELD-READINESS AUDIT

**Auditor:** Curriculum Design Expert & School Sports Apparel Sales Expert
**Date:** August 1, 2026
**Scope:** All 6 TUF Academy TAE Training Manuals (ACAD-101 through ACAD-106)
**Purpose:** Assess field-readiness and sales competency before rep deployment

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The 6 training manuals represent an **impressively thorough curriculum** with strong sales DNA — coach-to-coach voice, verbatim scripts, competitive positioning, and practical frameworks (SPIN, 3-Call Close, 3-Thing Close). The philosophy is coherent, the brand voice is authentic, and the operational detail (handoff checklists, sizing sessions, QA protocols) is excellent.

**However, there are 7 cross-cutting structural gaps that prevent the manuals from being fully field-ready.** Three are critically urgent: (1) the "Beyond Certification" section is buried at the end of every manual instead of at the top where lazy reps who skim will see it; (2) five of six manuals lack FAQ banks; and (3) emojis appear in three manuals violating the brand standard. Four additional gaps — incomplete role-play counts, missing founder story mentions, absent sales philosophy cross-references, and the missing BSN pricing anchor — weaken field preparation but are less urgent.

**Bottom Line:** With the 7 prioritized fixes below, these manuals go from "excellent training documents" to "career-long field weapons that even the laziest rep can use effectively."

## PRIORITIZED FIX LIST — TOP 10 MOST CRITICAL GAPS

These are ordered by impact on field-readiness. A rep who encounters these gaps will be less prepared to sell.

### FIX 1 [CRITICAL] — Move "Beyond Certification" to the TOP of all 6 manuals

**Problem:** Every manual places "USING THIS MANUAL BEYOND CERTIFICATION" at the very end (after Appendices, after quiz questions). The standard explicitly states it must be at the top — "even lazy reps who skim must see it." Currently, a rep who skims the manual will NEVER see it.

**Affected:** All 6 manuals. Manual 01 line 619, 02 line 744, 03 line 705, 04 line 720, 05 line 670, 06 line 857.

**Fix:** Cut the "Beyond Certification" section from the end of each manual and paste it immediately after the Table of Contents, before Part 1. This section tells the rep WHEN and HOW to use the manual during their actual sales week — it belongs at the front.

**Estimated effort:** 30 minutes for all 6 manuals.

---

### FIX 2 [CRITICAL] — Add FAQ Banks to Manuals 01-05

**Problem:** Only Manual 06 (Product Knowledge) has a FAQ bank (52 Q&As). Manuals 1-5 have zero FAQ sections. Coaches ask questions during prospecting, discovery, proposal, and handoff — reps need prepared answers.

**Affected:** Manuals 01, 02, 03, 04, 05.

**Fix:** Add FAQ sections to each manual:
- **Manual 01 (Philosophy):** 15-20 Q&As. "What makes TUF different?" "Are you new?" "Why should I switch?" "What's the catch?" "How do I know you'll be around?"
- **Manual 02 (Prospecting):** 15-20 Q&As. "How did you get my number?" "Why are you calling me?" "Do you work on commission?" "What's the smallest order you do?"
- **Manual 03 (Discovery):** 15-20 Q&As. Common coach questions during discovery that the rep should have answers for without derailing the SPIN flow.
- **Manual 04 (Proposal):** 15-20 Q&As. "What if I need more time?" "Can I customize further?" "What about rush orders?" "Who handles artwork?"
- **Manual 05 (Order Handoff):** 15-20 Q&As. "When will my order arrive?" "Can I add items?" "What if sizes are wrong?" "How do returns work?" "Can I talk to production directly?"

**Estimated effort:** 2-3 hours to draft all 5 FAQ banks.
